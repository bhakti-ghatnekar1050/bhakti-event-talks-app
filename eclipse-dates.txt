The next total solar eclipses worldwide are:

*   August 12, 2026: Greenland, Iceland, Spain, Russia, and Portugal.
*   August 2, 2027: North Africa (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya, Egypt), Middle East (Saudi Arabia, Yemen), and Southern Spain.
*   July 22, 2028: Australia and New Zealand.
*   November 25, 2030: Botswana, South Africa, and Australia.
*   November 14, 2031: Pacific Ocean.
*   March 30, 2033: Alaska (USA) and Russia.
*   March 20, 2034: Central Africa and South Asia.
*   September 1–2, 2035: China, North Korea, Japan, and the Pacific Ocean.

For the contiguous United States, the next total solar eclipse will not occur until August 22, 2044 (visible in Montana and North Dakota), followed by a coast-to-coast eclipse on August 12, 2045.
